# 5. Stakeholders

## 5.1 List of Stakeholders

The stakeholders and the problems they feel. Names of the people in each role will be added once the team confirms its contacts.

| **Stakeholder** | **Pain today** | **Problems** |
|---|---|---|
| Security / control-room operator | Watches raw video; sees incidents only in review. | P1, P2 |
| Safety / operations manager | Relies on paper logs and spreadsheets; cannot quantify risk or produce audit data. | P1, P2 |
| Facility owner | Faces accident cost and compliance exposure; cannot afford commercial platforms. | P1, P3 |
| IT / system administrator | Has no single place to configure zones, schedules and access. | P2 |
| Auditor / regulator | Needs traceable evidence under OSHA, ISO 45001 and Egyptian law. | P1 |
| Supervisor and examiners | Need a feasible, scoped, demonstrable build. | P3 |

## 5.2 Roles and Responsibilities

The role of each stakeholder in the project and in the system:

| **Stakeholder** | **Role and responsibilities** |
|---|---|
| Security / control-room operator | Reviews alerts and decides on each one (confirm, dismiss or escalate). |
| Safety / operations manager | Reviews the risk picture and reports, follows up on escalated alerts, and approves reports before they are issued. |
| Facility owner | Sponsors adoption and decides which safety services the site uses. |
| IT / system administrator | Connects cameras; configures zones, schedules, rule types and user access. |
| Auditor / regulator | Reviews evidence and reports for compliance. |
| Supervisor and examiners | Approve the scope and evaluate the end-to-end demo. |
