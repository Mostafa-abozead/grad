# 3. Current Business Environment

## 3.1 Current Processes

Small and mid-sized factories and warehouses run security CCTV, safety logs and operations tracking as separate, manual systems. Security footage is reviewed only after an incident. Safety relies on manual checks, paper logs and spreadsheets. Commercial AI video analytics exist (for example Protex AI, Voxel, viAct) but are closed, expensive and region-specific, and AI-ready hardware is costly, so these facilities cannot adopt them. The target is a single factory or warehouse (or a lab mockup) with existing CCTV/IP cameras and no budget for new cameras or sensors.

## 3.2 Challenges and Issues

Violations such as missing PPE, intrusion into hazardous zones, unprotected work at height and unsafe machine proximity are found at audits or after incidents. Facilities lack the data to meet reporting obligations and have no unified picture of risk. The core problems are:

| **ID** | **Core problem** | **Consequence** |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Reactive safety monitoring. Security footage is reviewed after an incident; safety relies on manual checks, paper logs and spreadsheets. | Delayed response; violations surface only at audits or after accidents; regulatory data cannot be produced. |
| P2 | No unified visibility. CCTV, safety logs and operational tracking never communicate. | No single view of what is happening on the floor or of facility risk. |
| P3 | Cost of upgrading infrastructure. Commercial AI video analytics (for example Protex AI, Voxel, viAct) are closed, expensive and region-specific; AI-ready hardware is costly. | Small and mid-sized facilities cannot adopt them; no generic, configurable, education-grade alternative exists. |

## 3.3 Market Demand Signals

A snapshot of 23 public Upwork and Freelancer.com listings (October 26, 2026) shows recurring demand for PPE detection, fixed-camera video analytics, object tracking, event filtering and natural-language interfaces to monitoring data. The sample is qualitative: it indicates what clients ask for, not market size or willingness to pay.
