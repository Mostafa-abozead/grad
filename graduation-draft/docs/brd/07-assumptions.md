# 7. Assumptions

## 7.1 List of Assumptions

The following assumptions were made during planning:

| **ID** | **Assumption** |
|---|---|
| A-01 | Existing cameras give video of usable quality and a stable view of the monitored area. |
| A-02 | The target is one facility or a lab mockup. |
| A-03 | Labelled or sample footage is available for training and testing. |
| A-04 | Compute is available on-premises, in the cloud, or at the edge; the final deployment model will be confirmed (OD-01). |
