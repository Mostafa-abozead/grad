# What We Offer & How We Differ

## What we offer

An **AI-powered industrial safety and surveillance system** that converts existing RGB camera feeds into **real-time, structured safety events and auditable risk information** — with **no additional hardware**. Small and mid-sized factories and warehouses already own the cameras; what they lack is a way to act on what those cameras see. We close that gap with three layers:

1. **Computer-vision pipeline** — detects people, vehicles, and hazards on the existing CCTV/IP feeds.
2. **Config-driven rules engine** — converts detections into site-specific safety events: restricted-zone intrusion, missing PPE, fire/smoke, proximity, worker location.
3. **Output layer** — delivers the events through instant alerts, a risk dashboard, and an agentic-AI assistant, plus an evidence trail for audits, insurance, and regulators.

The architecture is deliberately **pluggable and API-driven**: every service is an independent unit integrated through a shared event schema — a generic, config-driven platform rather than a siloed point solution. The scope is **camera-only** — no thermal cameras and no radio/beacon personnel tagging — which keeps the system affordable for the target market and achievable within an academic project cycle.

**Our target: small and mid-sized factories and warehouses** — facilities that already have CCTV but no safety-AI team or budget; commercial platforms (Protex, Voxel, Intenseye) are closed, expensive, and priced for large enterprises, while hardware-based alternatives are out of reach for this segment.

## What makes us different

**No new hardware — camera-only, pluggable, API-driven, config-driven per site without code.**

| Differentiator | What it means |
|---|---|
| **No hardware** | Camera-only scope: works with the existing CCTV/IP feeds — no thermal cameras, RFID/UWB/BLE tags, or wearables; nothing per-worker to buy, install, or charge. |
| **Pluggable & API-driven** | Every service is an independent unit integrated through a shared event schema — connects to existing CCTV/VMS and safety workflows in days, no rip-and-replace. |
| **Config-driven** | Site rules (zones, speeds, PPE, vehicle types) are set by the customer, not hard-coded — a generic platform, not a siloed point solution; the same pattern Protex AI proves with its rule builder. |
| **Structured events, not boxes** | A detector that draws boxes is not a safety product — we produce real-time, structured, actionable safety events and auditable risk information. |
| **Evidence-first** | Every event carries time, location, camera, and clip — audit-ready output for regulators, insurers, and management. |
| **Honest by design** | Reports uncertainty and coverage gaps instead of implying complete observation — detections come with confidence, not false certainty. |
| **Right-sized for the market** | Built for small and mid-sized factories — the segment global platforms don't serve and hardware-based systems can't reach. |
