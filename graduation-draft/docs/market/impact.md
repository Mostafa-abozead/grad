# Impact Research — The Cost of Workplace Injuries & Documented Outcomes of AI Video Analytics

## Purpose & method

This is a research document collecting **published articles, blogs, and case studies** that quantify what AI video analytics **demonstrably changes when deployed** — documented, named-customer outcomes from providers in our [benchmark](./companies/README.md) and beyond, plus the ROI/business-case literature.

**The problem side of the ledger** — what workplace injuries cost (NSC $176.5B, Liberty Mutual $58.7B, CAPMAS Egypt) — is researched separately in [`../incidents/impact.md`](../incidents/impact.md).

Sources were selected by: named customer, quantified outcome, timeframe stated, and public URL. Every outcome figure is **vendor-reported, not independently verified** — flagged in [Caveats](#6-caveats-read-this-before-quoting-numbers). Anchor references are the two blogs that started this research: the [Mefron/Mikshi case study](https://mikshi.mantratec.com/case-studies/mefron-technologies-ppe-compliance-ai-video-analytics) and the [AvidBeam PPE-detection article](https://www.avidbeam.com/why-personal-protective-equipment-detection-is-the-future-of-workplace-safety).

---

## 1. The problem being solved, in numbers (summary)

Full cost research: [`../incidents/impact.md`](../incidents/impact.md). Headlines:

- **$176.5B** total cost of work injuries (NSC, US 2023) — **$43,000 per medically consulted injury**, ~3× more in indirect costs.
- **$58.74B** direct employer cost, >$1B/week (Liberty Mutual WSI 2026) — with falls, struck-by, and slips (causes 2–5) alone at **$25B**, all camera-detectable event classes.
- **Egypt (CAPMAS 2024):** 8,091 injuries, manufacturing 45.6% of cases, falls + collisions ≈ 60%, 207,688 absence days.

The rest of this document answers: *what happens when the fix is deployed?*

## 4. Detection-scale outcomes (leading indicators)

These show *why* the incident reductions happen — AI catches orders of magnitude more precursor events than humans can:

- **200× more hazards** identified by AI than manual observation at a US food manufacturer — 597 AI-flagged hazards vs **3** documented manually; **61% ↓** hazard detections in 2022 ([Intenseye case study](https://www.intenseye.com/case-studies/catch-200x-more-hazards-with-leading-indicator-data)).
- Static-delimination alerts falling from **12,216 → 89** within months as coaching and fixes land (Japanese beverage mfr, Intenseye).
- **Terex Utilities** (Visionify): 4,658 safety events logged in 6 weeks across 20 zones — 2,820 zone/pathway events, 1,546 PPE violations broken down by type; 95% ↓ forklift speed violations ([case study](https://visionify.ai/case-studies/terex-construction-safety)).

## 6. Caveats — read this before quoting numbers

1. **All outcome figures are vendor-reported, not independently verified.** Case studies are published by the providers themselves.
2. **Self-selected customers** — providers publish their best deployments; failures are not documented.
3. **Metric ambiguity** — many figures measure *reductions in detected unsafe events* (leading indicators), not injuries. A 73% drop in PPE alerts ≠ a 73% drop in injuries, though the causal chain is plausible.
4. **Baseline effects** — alert counts often spike at deployment (AI learning period) before falling; "reduction" percentages are measured against that early peak.
5. Use ranges, not single numbers: across ~30 deployments collected here, **typical reported outcomes are 40–90% reductions in unsafe behaviours/events and 20–50% reductions in recordable injuries**, usually landing within **30–90 days** — consistent with viso.ai's aggregated 25–40% accident-reduction claim.

## 7. What this means for our project

- **The problem is expensive and measurable** — see [`../incidents/impact.md`](../incidents/impact.md): $176.5B total / $58.7B direct (US 2023), ~4% of global GDP, and locally 8,091 injuries + 207,688 absence days.
- **The intervention is proven across ~30 named deployments** on exactly our MVP event classes: PPE (41–95% ↓), zone/access control (46–92% ↓), housekeeping/blocked exits (71% ↓), vehicle–pedestrian risk (50–98% ↓).
- **The economics work on existing CCTV** — no new hardware in nearly every case above; the "80% lower investment by using existing CCTV" claim (Superb AI) is precisely our [no-hardware thesis](../project_overview/what_we_offer.md).
- **The gap is access, not capability:** every deployment above is enterprise-scale (Henkel, US Steel, NSG, Bendix). Egypt's SME factories are priced out of these platforms — the gap [we target](../project_overview/what_we_offer.md).
