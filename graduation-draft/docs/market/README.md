# Market Research

## Purpose

This folder holds market context that supports the BRD without turning the BRD into a market study. It records what buyers and clients are asking for in comparable computer-vision projects, which providers operate in this space (globally and in Egypt), who has adopted these systems in Egypt, and how that evidence maps to the current requirements.

## Method and Limitations

- **Listing snapshot:** 23 public listings collected on October 26, 2026 from Upwork and Freelancer.com — see [Job Listings](./job_listings/README.md) for method details.
- **Provider research:** based on public company websites, case-study pages, press coverage, and LinkedIn profiles, collected November 2026. Vendor-reported impact figures are labeled as reported, not independently verified.
- **Use:** qualitative evidence of demand patterns, common scopes, skill expectations, and the competitive/adopters landscape.
- **Not:** a market-size study, pricing benchmark, or proof of willingness to pay.
- Listings and links can change or expire; budgets and experience levels are recorded as displayed at collection time.
- **CAPMAS:** the work-injury note uses official formal-sector statistics; it supports problem framing and sector prioritization, not demand or willingness-to-pay claims.
