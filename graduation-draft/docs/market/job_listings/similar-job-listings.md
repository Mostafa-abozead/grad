# Similar Job Listings and Projects

## Purpose

This catalog records public freelance listings that resemble the project's scope: computer vision for safety, surveillance, PPE compliance, video analytics, object detection and natural-language monitoring interfaces. It is qualitative market evidence, not a market-size study.

The summary used by the BRD is [Appendix B: Market Evidence](../brd/appendix-b-market-evidence.md).

## Snapshot and Method

- **Collected:** October 26, 2026.
- **Sources:** Upwork and Freelancer.com.
- **Count:** 23 listings.
- **Search terms:** computer vision, YOLO, OpenCV, PPE detection, surveillance, object detection, video analytics, edge AI, monitoring interface.
- **Selection:** listings that either match part of the current BRD scope or show a nearby technical/business pattern.
- **Relevance groups:** directly relevant, adjacent computer vision, and outside the current technical scope.

## Implications

### What This Validates

- There is visible demand for PPE detection, fixed-camera video analytics, surveillance event detection and natural-language monitoring interfaces.
- The AI assistant being `Must` is consistent with the market: buyers ask for natural-language access to monitoring data.
- Tracking, duplicate filtering and low-latency/edge inference are recurring technical themes.
- The four-core scope is commercially legible: PPE and surveillance are the clearest direct matches.

### What This Does Not Validate

- These are freelance listings, not enterprise procurement contracts. They do not establish market size, pricing or willingness to pay.
- Several listings are generic search pages, and some links may expire.
- The listings do not prove that a single facility will buy all four core services.
- Face recognition and LiDAR demand exists but does not justify expanding the current scope.

## Limitations

- Snapshot of 23 listings only; it is not a systematic sample.
- Budgets and experience levels are recorded as displayed and are not normalized across platforms.
- Some listings are search-result pages rather than individual job posts.
- Descriptions are summarized; the original listing is the source of truth.
- No inference should be drawn about the Egyptian or MENA market from these listings alone.
