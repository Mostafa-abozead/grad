# Job Listings — Market Evidence

## Purpose

This folder holds the collected job-listing and project-posting evidence: what clients on freelance marketplaces were asking for in computer-vision surveillance and safety projects at the time of collection. It supports the market-research claims in the [Market Research overview](../README.md) and the BRD's [Appendix B: Market Evidence](../../brd/appendix-b-market-evidence.md).

## Contents

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Similar Job Listings and Projects](./similar-job-listings.md) | Structured snapshot of 23 Upwork and Freelancer.com listings, grouped by relevance, with budgets, required skills, and mapping to BRD requirements. |
| [job listings.pdf](./job%20listings.pdf) | Raw PDF export of the listings at collection time (source evidence for the structured snapshot). |

## Method

- **Snapshot date:** October 26, 2026.
- **Sources:** Upwork and Freelancer.com.
- **Selection:** keyword searches for computer vision, YOLO, OpenCV, PPE detection, surveillance, object detection, video analytics, and related terms.
- **Use:** qualitative evidence of demand patterns, common project scopes, and skill expectations.

## Limitations

- A point-in-time snapshot — listings and links can change or expire.
- Budgets and experience levels are recorded as displayed at collection time, not normalized.
- This is **not** a market-size study, pricing benchmark, or proof of willingness to pay; it shows what a sample of clients asked for, not what a market pays.
