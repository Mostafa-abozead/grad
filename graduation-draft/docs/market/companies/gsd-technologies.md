## What they do

GSD Technologies turns existing camera networks into proactive safety and quality tools. Its pitch: maximize the value of installed CCTV with turnkey video analytics that automate HSE compliance, detect hazards instantly, and safeguard assets — with **fully on-premise deployment** so facility data never leaves the customer's infrastructure.

## Services & capabilities

- **Carrot Vision AI safety platform** (exclusive Egypt agency) — see [`carrot-vision.md`](carrot-vision.md): 14+ industrial AI models over existing CCTV.
  - **PPE compliance** — hard hat, high-visibility vest, glove detection (claimed 99.2% accuracy, including low-light environments; edge-native model).
  - **Fire & smoke** — early-stage detection of smoke plumes and open flames, claimed 5× faster than traditional hardware sensors.
  - **Face recognition** — attendance tracking and unauthorized-personnel detection with whitelist/blacklist support.
  - **Restricted zones / crane zones** — real-time alerts when personnel enter areas they shouldn't, including monitoring crowding beneath active overhead cranes.
  - **Perimeter trespassing**, cigarette detection in fuel/generator areas (with look-alike filtering, e.g. vs. eating or phone use), thermal-camera integration for high-temperature kiln areas, item/segment counting for material-flow visibility.
- **QHSE consulting ecosystem** — turns passive surveillance into real-time operational intelligence; also builds custom CV models and contact-center QA agents.

## Adopters (Egypt)

- **Three cement factories** operated by the world's **second-largest cement group** — plant-wide PPE compliance, restricted-zone monitoring, fire & smoke, thermal-camera integration, segment counting; a single AI backbone running consistently across the three sites for group-level safety visibility.
- **Precast-concrete mega-project sites** (delivered with contractor MCC) — see [`carrot-vision.md`](carrot-vision.md).
- **Mechanical workshop deployments** — PPE (hardhat/vest) and fire/smoke detection tuned to avoid false alarms from welding sparks and grinding; coverage mapping against the workshop floor plan before installation.

## Differentiators

- Fully **on-premise**, no proprietary hardware overhaul — plugs into existing CCTV networks.
- Localization-first: coverage/accuracy mapping done on the customer's floor plan before deployment.
- Industry focus mirrors Egypt's heavy sectors: cement, construction & building materials, oil & gas.
