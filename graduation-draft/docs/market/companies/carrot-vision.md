## What they do

Carrot Vision deploys on a customer's current CCTV systems and acts as a 24/7/365 safety supervisor: continuous oversight that eliminates human fatigue, real-time identification of PPE non-compliance, hazardous conditions, and unauthorized access — turning passive video feeds into actionable intelligence.

## Services & capabilities

- **PPE detection** — helmets, vests, and other mandated equipment, flagged the moment it's missing.
- **Restricted zones** — including dedicated **crane-zone monitoring** that prevents unauthorized human presence around operating cranes.
- **Fire & smoke detection** — early, intelligent alerting before danger escalates.
- **Face recognition** — manages entry/exit and identifies individuals for access discipline and accountability.
- **Instant response ("The Brain")** — the system doesn't just detect, it interacts:
  - Immediate alerts to management via **email and WhatsApp**.
  - **PLC integration** with industrial control panels for automatic on-site actions when a hazard is detected.

## Adopters (Egypt)

- **Major precast-concrete manufacturer** working on one of Egypt's largest national projects, delivered as an end-to-end "smart site" package with contractor **MCC**:
  - Network/infrastructure design and camera deployment covering the full site.
  - PPE detection (helmet + vest), crane restricted zones, face recognition for entry/exit, fire & smoke detection.
  - WhatsApp/email alerts plus PLC-driven automatic site response.

## Differentiators

- One of the few Egyptian-market products with **PLC integration** for automated on-site intervention (a capability otherwise seen in global leaders like Intenseye's Hard Stop).
- Deployed and supported in Egypt exclusively through **GSD Technologies** (see [`gsd-technologies.md`](gsd-technologies.md)), which handles infrastructure, integration, and delivery.
- Very young company — proven in at least one flagship mega-project, but limited public track record beyond it.
