# viAct.ai — Service Catalog

**Reference (all services):** [https://www.viact.ai](https://www.viact.ai)

---

## 1. Computer Vision Platform (Video Analytics Solution)
**Reference:** [https://www.viact.ai/video-analytics-solution](https://www.viact.ai/video-analytics-solution)

The core product of viAct. It plugs into a site's existing IP CCTV cameras via RTSP (a standard protocol for streaming live camera footage — no new hardware needed) and uses AI computer vision to continuously watch the footage for safety breaches — PPE (personal protective equipment) non-compliance, unsafe behaviour, unauthorized zone entry, equipment misuse, and more — sending real-time alerts to supervisors via dashboard, email, or text message.

## 2. Vision AI Modules Hub (200+ scenario-based modules)
**Reference:** [https://www.viact.ai/aimodules](https://www.viact.ai/aimodules)

A marketplace/library of 200+ pre-trained AI detection "modules" that plug into the Computer Vision Platform, each covering a specific safety or productivity scenario. Key module groups include:
- **PPE (personal protective equipment) Detection** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/ppedetection)) — detects missing or improper use of helmets, vests, gloves, masks, and boots.
- **Height Work Safety** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/work-at-height-safety-software)) — flags missing harnesses, unsafe scaffold access, and edge/fall risks.
- **Danger Zone Safety** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/dangerzonedetection)) — draws virtual exclusion zones and alerts on unauthorized entry.
- **Confined Space Safety** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/confined-space-safety-monitoring)) — tracks headcount, entry duration, and compliance in confined vessels/spaces.
- **Area Control** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/solutions/area-control-safety-system)) — monitors human and vehicle movement across restricted or high-risk zones.
- **Behavioural Safety** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/solutions/behaviour-based-safety-software)) — detects unsafe acts like running, phone use, or smoking on site.
- **Vehicle Control** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/solutions/vehicle-control-management-software)) — monitors vehicle behaviour, blind spots, and near misses.
- **Housekeeping** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/solutions/housekeeping-assessment-software-solution)) — assesses site tidiness and material/waste management.
- **Ergonomics** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/solutions/ergonomics-assessment-software)) — analyzes worker posture and movement to reduce strain injuries.
- Plus dozens of use-case modules such as fire/smoke detection, weapon detection, fatigue detection, loitering, illegal parking, number-plate recognition, and worker-under-suspended-load monitoring.

## 3. viHUB (Centralized Management Platform / Digital Works Supervision System)
**Reference:** [https://www.viact.ai/vihub](https://www.viact.ai/vihub)

viAct's cloud (or on-prem/hybrid) dashboard that acts as the single source of truth for a project. Every camera feed, IoT (internet-connected sensor device) reading, AI module detection, and permit record streams into viHUB automatically — no manual uploading or logging — giving EHS (Environment, Health & Safety) teams one platform to view compliance data, generate reports, and manage safety across multiple sites. Also referred to as the project's Digital Works Supervision System (a construction-industry term for a digital site-oversight platform) and, in some materials, the Project Control Center.

## 4. viGENT (Agentic/Generative AI Safety Co-Pilot)
**Reference:** [https://www.viact.ai/generative-ai-solutions](https://www.viact.ai/generative-ai-solutions)

An LLM (large language model, the technology behind chatbots like ChatGPT)-powered conversational AI assistant built specifically for safety and operations teams, offering 300+ specialized AI agents. viGENT ingests live video, sensor data, and safety documents to autonomously monitor sites 24/7, interpret AI-generated alerts, answer safety queries, generate reports, and recommend corrective actions — acting as an always-on "safety co-pilot" layered on top of the video analytics platform.

## 5. Hardware / Physical AI Devices
**Reference:** [https://www.viact.ai/vilid](https://www.viact.ai/vilid) · [viAER](https://www.viact.ai/viaer) · [viMOV](https://www.viact.ai/vimov) · [viHOI](https://www.viact.ai/vihoi) · [viMAC](https://www.viact.ai/vimac) · [viBOT](https://www.viact.ai/vibot)

A line of physical edge-AI hardware that extends viAct's monitoring beyond fixed cameras:
- **viLID** — 3D LiDAR (a laser-based distance-sensing technology) sensor for monitoring trench depth, edge movement, and unauthorized access.
- **viAER** — Drone-based AI monitoring for elevated, large-scale, or hard-to-reach areas.
- **viMOV** — A self-contained, portable on-site AI device that works off-grid (no power/internet needed) for remote or confined sites, running on-device video analysis for PPE, danger zones, and atmospheric hazards.
- **viHOI** — AI-powered hoist/crane monitoring that tracks load movement and danger-zone breaches during lifting operations.
- **viMAC** — An in-cabin, on-device AI anti-collision system for industrial vehicles (forklifts, cranes, dump trucks) that detects driver distraction and enforces speed/zone compliance without relying on internet connectivity.
- **viBOT** — An autonomous mobile robot that patrols dynamic sites, performing remote inspections and hazard detection where fixed cameras aren't practical.

## 6. viWEAR — Wearables
**Reference:** [Smart Helmet](https://www.viact.ai/iot/smart-helmet) · [Smart Watch](https://www.viact.ai/iot/smart-watch)

Internet-connected wearables for individual worker monitoring:
- **Smart Helmet** — Combines GPS (satellite location tracking), fall/motionless detection, and hazard alerts in a standard-looking hard hat, giving safety teams live worker location and status.
- **Smart Watch** — An industrial safety watch that captures health vitals, movement, and GPS location (works even without network connectivity), used heavily for lone-worker monitoring and emergency escalation.

## 7. Permit to Work Software (PTW / ePTW — "electronic Permit to Work")
**Reference:** [https://www.viact.ai/permit-to-work-software](https://www.viact.ai/permit-to-work-software)

A digital permit-to-work system that replaces paper-based authorizations, ensuring only trained and approved personnel can access high-risk zones or perform hazardous tasks, with live status tracking integrated into viHUB.

## 8. Smart Site Safety System ("4S")
**Reference:** [https://www.viact.ai/smart-site-safety-system](https://www.viact.ai/smart-site-safety-system)

A bundled, plug-and-play safety package (built around the "Safety, Security, Sustainability, Smartness" model — hence "4S") combining the Computer Vision Platform, a centralized management dashboard, and the digital permit-to-work/works-supervision tools — designed for construction sites (with pricing around USD 200 per camera/module/month) and used to meet regulatory safety-system mandates in Hong Kong.

## 9. Red Zone Management
**Reference:** [https://www.viact.ai/red-zone-monitoring](https://www.viact.ai/red-zone-monitoring)

An AI and real-time monitoring solution that identifies and alerts workers when they enter designated high-risk "red zones" — areas with heavy machinery, crane operations, or other elevated hazards — commonly used on oil & gas and industrial sites.

## 10. IoT Device Suite
**Reference:** [https://www.viact.ai/iot](https://www.viact.ai/iot)

A set of standalone IoT sensors that feed into viHUB alongside the video analytics platform:
- **Smart Lock** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/iot/smart-lock)) — Remotely controlled locks tied to active work permits; access points can be locked/unlocked automatically when a permit is issued or revoked.
- **Weather Station** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/iot/weather-station)) — Tracks temperature, humidity, wind speed, and air quality with AI-driven forecasting and alerts.
- **Gas Leak Detector** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/iot/gas-leak-detection-system)) — Continuously monitors air quality/gas levels and sends real-time alerts to prevent fire, explosion, or toxic-exposure incidents.
- **Fleet Tracking System** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/iot/fleet-tracking-system)) — Monitors vehicle location, driver behaviour, maintenance needs, and fuel/emissions across industrial and commercial fleets.
- **Access Control System** ([link](https://www.viact.ai/iot/access-control-system)) — Uses RFID (short-range radio ID cards/tags), BLE (Bluetooth-based proximity sensing), and facial recognition to restrict and log entry to secure or hazardous areas.
