# Voxel AI — Service Catalog

**Reference (all services):** [https://www.voxelai.com](https://www.voxelai.com)

Voxel is a San Francisco-based "site intelligence" company. Its core technology is AI computer vision (software that interprets what's happening in video footage) that plugs into a facility's existing IP (internet-connected) security cameras — no new hardware needed — to spot safety and operational risks in real time. That single underlying platform is then packaged into three customer-facing solutions, described below.

---

## 1. Safety
**Reference:** [https://www.voxelai.com/solutions-safety](https://www.voxelai.com/solutions-safety)

Gives enterprise safety teams real-time visibility into workplace hazards before they turn into injuries. Voxel's AI continuously scans existing camera feeds for risks involving people, vehicles, equipment, and the physical environment — things like missing PPE (personal protective equipment), poor ergonomics (unsafe lifting/bending postures), speeding forklifts, near-misses, spills, and blocked exits — then routes each finding into a workflow where a manager can assign an owner, set a deadline, and track resolution. Executive-level reporting then shows leadership where risk exists and whether corrective actions are working.

## 2. Operations
**Reference:** [https://www.voxelai.com/solutions-operations](https://www.voxelai.com/solutions-operations)

Aimed at operations leaders (warehouse, distribution center, manufacturing plant, cold storage, and port/terminal managers) rather than safety teams specifically. It uses the same camera-based AI to catch process gaps that hurt throughput and margin: confirming standard operating procedures are actually being followed, automatically documenting damage events (what happened, where, and when), flagging loss-prevention issues like spoilage/theft/unauthorized access, and analyzing workflow or space usage to show where time and capacity are being wasted.

## 3. Risk Management
**Reference:** [https://www.voxelai.com/solutions-risk-management](https://www.voxelai.com/solutions-risk-management)

Built for enterprise risk and insurance teams. It surfaces near-misses and unsafe behaviors before they become recordable incidents, ties ergonomic/PPE/vehicle hazards to potential liability and workers'-compensation costs, and turns detected trends into documented, audit-ready evidence — useful for compliance reporting, insurance claims, and demonstrating measurable risk reduction over time.

---

### Underlying platform capabilities (apply across all three solutions)
- **Camera compatibility & fast deployment** — Works with roughly 95% of existing IP cameras and can typically be stood up at a new site within 48 hours, with no new camera hardware required.
- **Connect → Detect → Act → Prove workflow** — Voxel frames its product around four steps: centralize visibility across all sites (**Connect**), continuously flag risk in real time (**Detect**), convert each detection into an assigned task with a deadline (**Act**), and generate executive-level reporting to demonstrate ROI (**Prove**).
- **Privacy-by-design** — No facial recognition; faces and bodies are automatically blurred. The platform is SOC 2 Type II certified (an independent audit standard for how a vendor secures customer data), uses end-to-end encryption, supports role-based access control and SSO (single sign-on), encrypts data both in transit (TLS 1.2) and at rest (AES-256), and runs on ISO 27001-certified (an international data-security standard) data centers. It is also GDPR-ready (compliant with the EU's General Data Protection Regulation privacy law).
- **Industry focus** — Primarily serves retail, logistics & supply chain, and manufacturing customers, with named clients including CVS Health, Albertsons, Dick's Sporting Goods, Lowe's, and The Port of Virginia.

## Related Resource: Webinar — AI, HOP & Compliance
**Reference:** [https://youtu.be/smUsJvfZYMQ](https://youtu.be/smUsJvfZYMQ)

A webinar hosted by Voxel, featuring Tim Page-Bottorff (ASSP — American Society of Safety Professionals) and Chris Skipper (Voxel), on integrating AI, HOP (Human and Organizational Performance, a safety framework focused on how work actually gets done rather than just rule-following), and compliance to modernize workplace safety. Key points covered:

- **The New Safety Lens** (6:32–7:18) — Argues safety is not just the absence of injury but the presence of capacity, pushing organizations beyond traditional compliance toward a proactive, data-informed approach.
- **Computer Vision AI (CVAI)** (9:38–11:45) — How Voxel's technology uses existing security cameras for 24/7, unbiased visibility into hazards like powered industrial truck (forklift) interactions and PPE (personal protective equipment) compliance.
- **Integrating HOP Principles** (12:06–12:54, 17:36–19:20) — HOP holds that error is inevitable, context drives behavior, and learning is vital; pairing AI with these principles helps identify hazards without defaulting to blame, building a stronger safety culture.
- **Real-World Application** (20:44–23:46) — A case study of Carlex, a glass manufacturer, showing how focusing on learning and process improvement (rather than punitive action) drove significant reductions in safety risk.
- **Moving Beyond Compliance** (15:02, 16:01–16:20) — Compliance is framed as "the floor, not the ceiling," with AI freeing up safety professionals to focus on strategic improvements instead of one-off, point-in-time observations.
