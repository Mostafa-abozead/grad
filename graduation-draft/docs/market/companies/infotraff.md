## What they do

InfoTraff grew out of production-grade machine vision: its team built Egypt's first national-scale computer-vision enforcement system before pivoting to a productized AI CCTV analytics platform. The system runs an autonomous agent per camera that **sees** (ingests every frame, tracks people/objects/vehicles/hazards 24/7), **reasons** (distinguishes staff from visitors, genuine violations from brief blind spots, critical from low-priority events), and **acts** (alerts, reports, and staff notifications dispatched in **under 2 seconds** with camera evidence attached — no human in the loop required).

## Services & capabilities

- **Manufacturing safety & quality** — PPE compliance (helmet, vest, glove), defect detection on the line, assembly-line/throughput counting, fire & smoke detection, blocked-aisle (egress) alerts, spillage detection with instant notifications.
- **Retail & F&B analytics** — foot traffic & dwell time, queue depth and wait analysis, shopper-vs-staff classification, conversion uplift and operational-delay reduction metrics.
- **Vehicle & dock activity** — monitoring at warehouses and loading zones.
- **ShopTalk (flagship product)** — an AI facility assistant: real-time dashboards customized per client (visitors, dwell, queues, zone heatmaps) with a conversational interface for on-demand facility insights; integrates with POS/ERP.
- **Alert routing** — Telegram, SMS, email, webhooks; REST API and open integrations with ERP, POS, and access control; dashboards updated in under a second.
- **Privacy-first architecture** — no faces stored, no identities tracked ("reads movement, not people"), GDPR-compliant out of the box.

- The only local provider with a **proven national-scale government CV deployment** (seatbelt detection).
- Deliberately **biometric-free** — anonymous analytics instead of face recognition, a contrast to AvidBeam/GSD/MAKKN.
- Strong commercialization: packaged edge device, plug-and-play integrations (ERP/POS/Telegram), and public client logos spanning industrial and retail groups.
