## What they do

MAKKN is an AI consulting and product company ("supercharging AI digital transformation") with four products: MAKKN Desk (AI customer support), MAKKN Docs (document intelligence), MAKKN Insight (sales & voice intelligence), and **MAKKN Guard** (AI vision & safety monitoring). Guard "watches over your facility so your safety team doesn't have to be everywhere at once."

## MAKKN Guard — services & capabilities

- **Smart detection system** — computer vision on existing camera feeds (no new hardware): real-time PPE compliance (helmets, gloves, boots), restricted-zone and pedestrian-walkway monitoring, unauthorized zone entry, unsafe behavior.
- **Forklift speed detection** — vehicle safety monitoring on the warehouse floor.
- **Pressure-gauge monitoring** — reading analog gauges via camera for equipment safety.
- **Violation notification system** — instant alerts via the customer's channel of choice: **SMS, email, WhatsApp**, addressed to the right person within minutes, not at the end of a shift.
- **Configurable safety requirements** — per-zone/per-camera rules so the system reflects actual site policies, not one-size-fits-all.
- **Face recognition** — identifies individuals involved in violations for accurate incident logging and targeted coaching (with access/privacy controls in the customer's hands).
- **Dashboard & insights** — live incident dashboard, historical trends, hotspots and heatmap analytics, exportable compliance reports (audit-ready records by zone, shift, and violation type).

## Adopters

- **No publicly named Guard clients yet.** The product launched in 2026; MAKKN positions it at manufacturers and warehouse operators across Egypt and MENA (its other products — Desk, Docs, Insight — target retail, healthcare, and real estate regionally).

## Differentiators

- Explicitly built for **WhatsApp-first alerting** and per-zone configurability — matched to how Egyptian factory supervisors actually work.
- Fastest to deploy among local options (software overlay on existing cameras; no edge appliance marketing).
- Early-stage: thin public proof compared to AvidBeam or GSD, but covers a broader detection menu (including forklift speed and gauge reading) than most local rivals.
