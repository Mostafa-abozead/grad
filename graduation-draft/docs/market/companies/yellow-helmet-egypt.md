## What they do

Yellow Helmet Egypt is an integrated HSSEQ (Health, Safety, Security, Environment & Quality) and industrial-transformation partner. Unlike the other local players, its core business is consulting, training, fire-safety systems, ISO implementation, and digital transformation — with **AI Monitoring & Intelligent Industrial Systems** as its flagship (7th) service ecosystem layered on top.

## Services & capabilities

- **AI CCTV & PPE compliance** — helmet, vest, and PPE detection on existing CCTV; restricted-zone monitoring; unsafe-behavior identification; real-time alerts and reports.
- **Anomaly & incident detection** — fire and smoke, falls and injury indicators, intrusion and perimeter events, violence and abnormal-movement alerts.
- **Vision-Language Models (VLM)** — context-aware event interpretation and scene understanding (interprets events and behaviors, not just isolated objects), reducing false positives and enabling recognition of complex scenarios — the most advanced AI framing among local providers.
- **Edge real-time AI + Vision AI command layer** — on-site inference with central dashboards, alerts, reports, and operational intelligence; robotics integration claimed.
- **HSSEQ transformation services** (the traditional business): gap analysis & audits, PTW / Control of Work, behavior-based safety, contractor safety, ISO 9001/14001/45001 systems, emergency-response and firefighting training, fire/CCTV/access-control installation, digital twins, IoT, predictive maintenance.

## Adopters & partnerships

- **Industrial client base (HSSEQ/training engagements):** BP, Shell, Eni, Total, Coca-Cola, Alstom, EzzSteel, Uber — displayed as clients; these engagements span training, audits, and management systems rather than necessarily AI-monitoring deployments.
- **Government approvals:** recognized partner for safety/quality programs across Egypt's petroleum sector (EGPC, EGAS, ECHEM, GANOPE).
- **RRSC partnership (2025):** strategic technical partnership with The Rob Rockefeller Standard Carbon LLC establishing the **Engineering & Sustainability Center Egypt** in New Cairo — a regional MENA hub for HSSEQ, decarbonization, and AI monitoring (500+ m², 30+ consultants + 15 engineers, ISO 27001/ATEX/IECEx capability stack).
- **"Safe Industry" initiative (Oct 2026):** launched with the British University in Egypt's Continuing Education College and Oriental Weavers/El Nasr group, hosted by the 10th of Ramadan Investors Association — targeting **~5,000 factories** (priority: 400 large/medium plants) with free gap-analysis visits, safety-culture development, and specialized training; positions Yellow Helmet as the delivery vehicle for mass AI-safety adoption across Egyptian industry.
